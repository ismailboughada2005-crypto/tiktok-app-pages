# Your Baby Shower Invitation: step by step

No coding needed. You fill in a form, download your invitation, and put it online with Canva. It takes about 10 minutes.

**What is in your download**

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `editor.html` | The editor. **Start here.** |
| `index.html` | The invitation itself, with sample details (the editor makes your own copy) |
| `INSTRUCTIONS.pdf` | This guide |

---

## Step 1: Open the editor

1. Use a computer (laptop or desktop) for this part.
2. Double-click **`editor.html`**. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox).
3. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Everything stays on your computer.

## Step 2: Fill in your details

Work down the numbered boxes on the left. Your invitation updates in the phone preview as you type.

- **Style**: pick **Baby Girl** (pink) or **Baby Boy** (blue).
- **Wording**: the title, the mom-to-be (or parents-to-be) and the hosts.
- **Envelope**: the name every guest sees on the envelope, for example "Friends & Family".
- **Photo** (optional): a bump photo or ultrasound replaces the teddy bear picture. It is made smaller for you so the invitation loads fast.
- **Place**: the address. The "Directions" button and the map use it.
- **Registry**: add a button for each registry (Amazon, Target, Babylist…) and paste its link.
- **Diaper raffle & books for baby**: fun extras you can turn on or off.
- **RSVP**: choose where replies go: your WhatsApp, your email, or your own form link (a Google Form keeps all replies in one list).
- Every section has a **Show** switch. Turn off what you do not need.
- Above the preview, **Envelope** shows the opening animation.

Your work saves itself in the browser. **Save draft** keeps a copy you can return to with **Load draft**. **Export draft file** makes a backup file you can **Import** later or on another computer. If your photo is very large, the browser may not have room to keep it: the editor tells you, and **Export draft file** always works.

## Step 3: Download

Press **Download my invitation**. A file called **`index.html`** lands in your Downloads folder.

## Step 4: Put it online with Canva

1. Go to **https://www.canva.com** and sign in (or create an account).
2. Drag **`index.html`** from your Downloads folder onto the Canva home page. It opens as a new design showing your invitation.
3. Click **Share** at the top right, then publish it as a website.
4. Canva gives you your link. Copy it.
5. Open the link on your phone and tap through the envelope once to check everything looks right.

## Step 5: Share

Paste your link into WhatsApp, a text message, an email or anywhere else. Guests tap it, the envelope opens, and they can reply from the RSVP section.

---

## Changing something later

1. Open `editor.html` again. Your draft is remembered.
2. Make your changes and press **Download my invitation** again.
3. Drag the new `index.html` into Canva and publish it the same way. If the link changes, send guests the new one.

---

## Questions

**Where do the RSVPs go?**
There is no account or database. When a guest presses send, their reply opens as a ready-written WhatsApp message or email addressed to you, and they press send. With "my own form link" guests are taken to your form.

**Can I add a photo?**
Yes, one photo at the top (for example a bump photo or ultrasound). Without a photo, a teddy bear with balloons is shown.

**Can I use my own music?**
Yes. In **12 · Closing, music & menu** choose **My own MP3 link** and paste a link that ends in `.mp3`. Make sure you have the right to use the song. The built-in music box lullabies (Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star and Brahms' Lullaby) are traditional melodies free to use. Music only plays after a guest taps the ♪ button.

**The countdown shows the wrong time.**
The invitation shows the date and time exactly as you typed them, on every guest's phone.

**I am comfortable with code. Can I edit the file directly?**
Yes. Open `index.html` in a text editor. All your details are in the `CONFIG` block near the bottom of the file, with a note on every line.

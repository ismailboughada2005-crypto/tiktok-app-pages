    ?How to publish & shareAbout 5 minutes, with Canva

    1. Download

    1. Press Download my invitation. A file called index.html lands in your Downloads folder.

    2. Put it online with Canva

    1. Go to canva.com and sign in (or create an account).
    2. Drag index.html from your Downloads folder onto the Canva home page. It opens as a new design showing your invitation.
    3. Click Share at the top right, then publish it as a website.
    4. Canva gives you your link. Copy it.
    5. Open the link on your phone and tap through the envelope once to check everything looks right.

    3. Share

    1. Paste your link in WhatsApp, a text message or an email.

    Need to change something later?

    Come back to this page (your draft is remembered), edit, and download again. Then drag the new index.html into Canva and publish it the same way. Send the new link if it changes.

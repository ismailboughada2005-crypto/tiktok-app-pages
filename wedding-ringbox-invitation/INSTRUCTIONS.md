# Your Wedding Ring Box Invitation: step by step

No coding needed. You fill in a form, download your invitation, and put it online with Canva. It takes about 10 minutes.

**What is in your download**

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `editor.html` | The editor. **Start here.** |
| `index.html` | The invitation itself, with sample details (the editor makes your own copy) |
| `INSTRUCTIONS.pdf` | This guide |

---

## Step 1: Open the editor

1. Use a computer (laptop or desktop) for this part.
2. Double-click **`editor.html`**. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox).
3. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Everything stays on your computer.

## Step 2: Fill in your details

Work down the numbered boxes on the left. Your invitation updates in the phone preview as you type.

- **Style & occasion**: pick one of the 5 themes (Ivory & Gold, Sage Garden, Dusty Blue, Blush Pink or Burgundy) and the occasion: Wedding, Wedding Reception, Elopement Celebration, Vow Renewal, Engagement Party or Rehearsal Dinner.
- **The couple & wording**: type your two first names. They appear big on the invitation and as initials in the illustration. The lines above and under your names are written for you; type your own if you like.
- **Your photo**: press **Choose a photo**. It is resized for you. With no photo, a floral arch illustration with your initials is shown.
- **RSVP**: choose where replies go: your WhatsApp, your email, or your own form link (for example a Google Form).
- Every optional section (order of the day, menu, registry, good to know and so on) has a **Show** switch. Turn off what you do not need.
- **Name on the ring box tag** is the name every guest sees, for example "Friends & Family". Leave it empty to show "You're Invited".
- **RSVP**: guests can also send a song that will get them dancing.
- Above the preview, **Opening** shows the ring box animation.

Your work saves itself in the browser. **Save draft** keeps a copy you can return to with **Load draft**. **Export draft file** makes a backup file you can **Import** later or on another computer.

## Step 3: Download

Press the gold **Download my invitation** button. A file called **`index.html`** lands in your Downloads folder.

## Step 4: Put it online with Canva

1. Go to **https://www.canva.com** and sign in (or create an account).
2. Drag **`index.html`** from your Downloads folder onto the Canva home page. It opens as a new design showing your invitation.
3. Click **Share** at the top right, then publish it as a website.
4. Canva gives you your link. Copy it.
5. Open the link on your phone and open the ring box once to check everything looks right.

## Step 5: Share

Paste your link into WhatsApp, a text message, an email or anywhere else. Guests tap it, the ring box opens, the ring sparkles, the card appears with falling rose petals, and they can reply from the RSVP section.

---

## Changing something later

1. Open `editor.html` again. Your draft is remembered.
2. Make your changes and press **Download my invitation** again.
3. Drag the new `index.html` into Canva and publish it the same way. If the link changes, send guests the new one.

## Making a second invitation

Hosting more than one party? Press **Export draft file** to keep the first one, then **Start over** and fill in the new details.

---

## Questions

**Where do the RSVPs go?**
There is no account or database. When a guest presses send, their reply opens as a ready-written WhatsApp message or email addressed to you, and they press send. With "my own form link" guests are taken to your form.

**Can I use my own music?**
Yes. In **12 · Music & sounds** choose **My own MP3 link** and paste a link that ends in `.mp3`. Make sure you have the right to use the song. The built-in music box plays Pachelbel's Canon in D, which is free to use. Music only plays after a guest taps the ♪ button.

**The countdown shows the wrong time.**
The invitation shows the date and time exactly as you typed them, on every guest's phone.

**I am comfortable with code. Can I edit the file directly?**
Yes. Open `index.html` in a text editor. All your details are in the `CONFIG` block near the bottom of the file, with a note on every line.

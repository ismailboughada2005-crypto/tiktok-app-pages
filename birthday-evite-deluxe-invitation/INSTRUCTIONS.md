# Your Animated Birthday Invitation: step by step

No coding needed. You fill in a form, download your invitation, and put it online for free. It takes about 10 minutes.

**What is in your download**

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `editor.html` | The editor. **Start here.** |
| `index.html` | The invitation itself, with sample details (the editor makes your own copy) |
| `INSTRUCTIONS.pdf` | This guide |

---

## Step 1: Open the editor

1. Use a computer (laptop or desktop) for this part.
2. Double-click **`editor.html`**. It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox).
3. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Everything stays on your computer.

## Step 2: Fill in your details

Work down the numbered boxes on the left. The phone preview on the right updates as you type.

- **Pick your theme**: Balloon Bash, Neon Glow, Sweet Cake, Golden Gala or Tropical Fiesta. Each one opens with its own animation: balloons float away, a disco ball lights up, candles are blown out, curtains part or a piñata cracks open.
- **The birthday star**: the name appears big in script. Add the age they are turning to show it as a big number (leave it empty to hide it).
- **Wording**: the headline ("It's a Party", "Let's Fiesta"…) and the line above the name are written for you. Type your own to change them.
- **Photo** (optional): press **Choose a photo**. It is resized for you.
- **Date, time & place**: date, start and end time, place name and address. The **Open in Maps** button uses the address, or paste your own Google Maps link.
- **Good to know**: a short note, for example a dress code or "Surprise party, keep it a secret!".
- **Hosted by**: who is throwing the party.
- **RSVP**: choose where replies go: WhatsApp, a text message (SMS), email, or your own form link (for example a Google Form). Add the "reply by" date.
- **Wish list** (optional): paste a gift link (Amazon wish list, etc.). Leave it empty to hide the button.
- **Closing message**: a fun note at the end. The age (or the first initial) is shown in a little badge.
- **Music**: a built-in Happy Birthday melody or party jingle, or paste a link to your own MP3. Music never starts by itself; guests tap the ♪ button.

Above the preview, **Opening** shows the opening animation. **Preview full screen** opens your invitation in a new tab, exactly as guests will see it.

Your work saves itself in the browser. **Save draft** keeps a copy you can return to with **Load draft**. **Export draft file** makes a backup you can open on another computer.

## Step 3: Download

Press **Download my invitation**. A file called **`index.html`** lands in your Downloads folder. Keep that exact name.

## Step 4: Put it online (free)

### Option A: Netlify Drop (easiest, about 2 minutes)

1. Make a new folder on your computer, for example `birthday`, and move **`index.html`** into it.
2. Go to **app.netlify.com/drop**.
3. Drag the whole folder onto the page. A few seconds later you get a link like `https://something.netlify.app`.
4. When Netlify asks, create a free account so your link stays online. You can pick a nicer name in **Site configuration → Change site name** (for example `mia-30th-birthday.netlify.app`).

### Option B: GitHub Pages

1. Sign in (or sign up free) at **github.com** and create a new **public** repository, for example `birthday`.
2. Click **Add file → Upload files**, drop **`index.html`**, then press **Commit changes**.
3. Open **Settings → Pages**. Under **Build and deployment** choose **Deploy from a branch**, branch **main**, folder **/ (root)**, then **Save**.
4. After about a minute your link appears at the top: `https://yourname.github.io/birthday/`.

## Step 5: Share your link

1. Open the link on your phone and tap through the opening once to check everything.
2. Send the link by text message, WhatsApp, email or social media.

**Tip:** add `?guest=Anna` to the end of your link to greet a guest by name, for example `https://mia-30th-birthday.netlify.app/?guest=Anna`.

## Changing something later

Open `editor.html` again (your draft is remembered), make the change and download again.

- **Netlify**: open your site, go to **Deploys**, and drag the folder with the new `index.html` onto the page.
- **GitHub**: upload the new `index.html` to the same repository (it replaces the old one).

Your link stays the same, so guests always see the latest version.

## Questions

**Do guests need an app?** No. The invitation opens in any phone browser.

**Where do RSVPs go?** To the WhatsApp number, phone (text message), email or form you chose. Guests tap send in their own app.

**Can I use my own song?** Yes. In **Music**, choose **My own MP3 link** and paste a direct link to an MP3 file you have the right to use.

**Does it work on computers too?** Yes. It is made for phones (320 to 480 px wide) and shows centred on a computer screen.

    ?How to publish & shareAbout 5 minutes, free

    1. Download

    1. Press Download my invitation. A file called index.html lands in your Downloads folder. Keep the name index.html.

    2. Put it online with Netlify Drop (easiest)

    1. Make a new folder on your computer, for example baby-shower, and move index.html into it.
    2. Go to app.netlify.com/drop.
    3. Drag the whole folder onto the page. In a few seconds you get a link like https://….netlify.app.
    4. Create a free Netlify account when it asks, so your link stays online. You can rename it in Site settings → Change site name.

    Or: GitHub Pages

    1. Sign in at github.com and create a new public repository.
    2. Click Add file → Upload files, drop index.html, then Commit changes.
    3. Open Settings → Pages, choose Deploy from a branch, branch main, folder / (root), and Save.
    4. After a minute your link appears at the top: https://yourname.github.io/repository-name/.

    3. Share

    1. Open the link on your phone and tap to open the invitation once to check it.
    2. Paste the link in a text message, WhatsApp or an email.

    Need to change something later?

    Come back to this page (your draft is remembered), edit, and download again. On Netlify, open your site, go to Deploys and drag the folder again. On GitHub, upload the new index.html over the old one. Your link stays the same.

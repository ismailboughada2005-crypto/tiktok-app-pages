    ?How to publish & shareAbout 5 minutes, free

    1. Download

    1. Press Download my invitation. A file called my-invitation.zip lands in your Downloads folder.
    2. Open it: on a Mac double-click it; on Windows right-click → Extract All. You now have a folder named my-invitation.

    2. Put it online with Netlify Drop (free)

    1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop.
    2. Drag the my-invitation folder onto the page and wait a few seconds.
    3. Netlify shows your link, something like https://jolly-elf-12345.netlify.app. Create the free account when it asks, so your invitation stays online.
    4. Optional: in Netlify, Site configuration → Change site name to get a nicer link such as hartwell-christmas.netlify.app.

    3. Turn on the picture in link previews

    1. Copy your link, paste it in Link preview → Your invitation link above, and download again.
    2. In Netlify open your site → Deploys, and drag the new folder onto the box at the bottom of the page.

    4. Share

    1. Paste your link in WhatsApp, a text message or an email.
    2. For a personal touch use Personal links for each guest above: each guest sees their own name on the envelope.

    Need to change something later?

    Come back to this page (your draft is remembered), edit, download, and drag the new folder onto Deploys in Netlify. Your link stays the same.
